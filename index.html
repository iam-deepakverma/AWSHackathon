About the Challenge

30 Hours to Build Something Meaningful.

The AWS Innovation Challenge is a beginner-friendly campus hackathon where students come together to identify real-world problems, develop innovative solutions, and showcase their technical skills.

⏱️

30-Hour Hackathon

Build, experiment, iterate and demonstrate your solution during the offline hackathon.

👥

Teams of 2–4

Collaborate with teammates and build something impactful together.

💡

Your Own Problem

Choose a theme and bring or develop your own problem statement.

☁️

Build with Technology

Explore cloud, AI and other technologies to turn your idea into a working prototype.

How It Works

From Idea to Impact.

Only 25 teams will participate in the offline hackathon. If registrations exceed the available capacity, an online evaluation will be conducted to shortlist the final teams.

1

Register

Form a team of 2–4 members and complete registration. Registration fee is ₹300 per team.

2

Online Evaluation

If more than 25 teams register, an online evaluation will be conducted for shortlisting.

3

Final 25 Teams

The final 25 teams will be shortlisted for the offline hackathon.

4

30-Hour Build

Shortlisted teams attend the campus hackathon and build their solution.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Who can participate?

B.Tech students from any branch can participate. Cross-year and cross-branch teams are encouraged.

What is the team size?

Each team must have 2–4 members.

How much is the registration fee?

The registration fee is ₹300 per team.

How many teams can participate offline?

The offline hackathon is limited to 25 teams.

What happens if more than 25 teams register?

An online evaluation round will be conducted to shortlist the final 25 teams.

How long is the hackathon?

The offline hackathon is 30 hours long.

Can we choose more than one theme?

No. Each team must select exactly one of the five themes.

Who provides the problem statement?

Each team must bring or develop its own problem statement based on the theme selected by the team.

Do we need previous AWS experience?

No. The hackathon is designed to be beginner-friendly.
